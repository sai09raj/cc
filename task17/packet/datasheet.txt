KINGSMEAD 33/11 kV PRIMARY SUBSTATION AND 11 kV NETWORK - DATA SHEET (sheet 1 of 2)

Arrangement (single-line diagram). The 33 kV grid infeed supplies the 33 kV busbar P33. Transformers T1 and T2 (33/11 kV) feed 11 kV busbar sections A and B, which are joined by the bus-section circuit breaker BS. Feeders F1 and F2 leave section A, feeders F3 and F4 leave section B. Secondary substations S1 to S12 are 11 kV nodes. Interconnectors IC-1 (S3 to S10) and IC-2 (S6 to S11) and the normally-open points NOP-1 (S4 to S8) and NOP-2 (S7 to S12) link the feeders. Every route, its cable sections, joints and lengths are on the cable route plan (sheet 3); each cable section has one cable type throughout.

33 kV grid infeed: a source of 33.6 kV (line) behind an impedance of 0.36 + j3.60 ohm per phase.

Transformers T1, T2: each 15 MVA, 33/11 kV, impedance 10.0 % on 15 MVA with X/R = 20, referred to the 11 kV side. On-load tap changer on the 33 kV winding, taps 1 to 17, tap 9 nominal; each tap step changes the 33 kV winding turns by 1.25 % of nominal; a higher tap number means more 33 kV turns (lower 11 kV voltage). The ratio at tap n is 33 (1 + 0.0125 (n - 9)) : 11 kV. Magnetising current and core loss are negligible.

Automatic voltage control (AVC). A transformer whose AVC is in AUTO runs on the tap that brings the voltage of the 11 kV busbar section it feeds closest to 11.20 kV, with every other tap position fixed as it is. In MANUAL the tap stays where the operator left it. When BS is closed both sections are one busbar.

Cables (11 kV, XLPE, aluminium, three-core), per phase at 90 C:
  type 300: R 0.130 ohm/km, X 0.093 ohm/km, rating 465 A
  type 185: R 0.211 ohm/km, X 0.097 ohm/km, rating 360 A
  type 95:  R 0.411 ohm/km, X 0.108 ohm/km, rating 245 A
  type 70:  R 0.568 ohm/km, X 0.112 ohm/km, rating 205 A
Cable shunt capacitance, switch and joint impedances are negligible. A closed route is a series impedance equal to the sum of its sections.

Loads at 09:00 (constant power, lagging power factor):
  S1 1450 kW pf 0.95 | S2 980 kW pf 0.93 | S3 1210 kW pf 0.94 | S4 760 kW pf 0.92
  S5 1630 kW pf 0.95 | S6 1120 kW pf 0.90 | S7 690 kW pf 0.91 | S8 1380 kW pf 0.95
  S9 1040 kW pf 0.93 | S10 850 kW pf 0.92 | S11 1720 kW pf 0.96 | S12 910 kW pf 0.93

Solar farm PV-1 at S10: available output at 09:00 is 4000 kW; it exports the lower of the available output and any export limit in force, at a fixed power factor of 0.95 absorbing reactive power (it draws reactive power from the network).

Capacitor bank C1 at S6: 1200 kvar at 11.0 kV, a fixed shunt capacitance (its kvar varies with the square of the S6 voltage).

Notes.
1. Report steady-state values for the network as the log leaves it at 09:00.
2. Switching state changes only through operations recorded in the log.
3. Voltages are line-to-line; currents are phase currents; power flows are three-phase.
4. Network losses = 33 kV infeed active power + PV-1 export - total load.
