CW-100 COOLING-WATER SYSTEM - OPERATING DATA SHEET

Arrangement. Pumps P-101A, P-101B and P-101C take suction from the open cooling-water sump (water level EL 0.0 m) and discharge in parallel, each through its own check valve, into the DN200 pump header L1, which runs to distribution node N2 (EL 4.0 m). From N2: line C1 through flow-control valve FV-101 and exchanger E-101 to an atmospheric drain at EL 6.0 m; line C2 through exchanger E-102 to an atmospheric drain at EL 5.0 m; and riser R to break tank TK-1, entering through a free discharge at EL 28.0 m (above any water level). TK-1 is open to atmosphere. Its overflow weir (crest EL 26.0 m) spills to the sump. From the TK-1 bottom outlet, gravity line G runs to node N4 (EL 3.0 m), which feeds line C3 (exchanger E-103, drain at EL 2.0 m), line C4 (manual valve HV-204 and exchanger E-104, drain at EL 7.0 m) and the outfall line, which rises over a pipe bridge (crest EL 21.5 m) and discharges to the river pond at EL -4.0 m. Pressure indicator PI-100 is at N2.

Pump curves (head H in m, flow Q in m3/s): H = H0 - k Q^2.
P-101A: H0 46.0, k 2600. P-101B: H0 44.0, k 2300. P-101C: H0 40.0, k 2900. Each pump's check valve and discharge fittings: K 4.0 on the DN150 velocity of that pump's flow. Pump suction losses are negligible.

Lines (inside diameter; length; total K of fittings, entrance and exit losses, excluding FV-101 and the exchangers):
L1 DN200 (202.7 mm) 85.0 m K 3.2 | C1 DN100 (102.3 mm) 40.0 m K 2.4 | C2 DN100 35.0 m K 2.1 | R DN150 (154.1 mm) 60.0 m K 2.6 |
G DN150 70.0 m K 1.9 | C3 DN80 (77.9 mm) 30.0 m K 2.2 | C4 DN80 25.0 m K 2.0 | Outfall up-leg N4 to the crest DN100 48.0 m K 1.6 (the K includes the elbow at the crest) |
Outfall down-leg crest to pond DN100 42.0 m K 1.3.

Exchangers (K on the line velocity): E-101 35, E-102 28, E-103 40, E-104 32.

FV-101 characteristic (K on the C1 line velocity versus opening): 20 % 410; 30 % 160; 45 % 52; 60 % 21; 80 % 8.5; 100 % 4.2.
FIC-101 in AUTO holds the C1 flow at its setpoint, 18.0 L/s, provided the valve can (it saturates fully open otherwise). In MANUAL the valve stays at the opening set by the operator.

Weir: rectangular, length 0.8 m, Q = 1.84 L h^1.5 (Q in m3/s, h the water level above the crest in m). When TK-1 is below the crest no water spills.

Friction: Darcy-Weisbach with Swamee-Jain, f = 0.25 / [log10(e/(3.7 D) + 5.74 / Re^0.9)]^2, roughness e 0.045 mm.
Water: density 998.2 kg/m3, kinematic viscosity 1.004e-6 m2/s, vapour pressure 2.339 kPa abs. Atmospheric pressure 101.325 kPa. g 9.81 m/s2.

General notes.
1. All heads and pressures are steady-state values. Free surfaces and drains are at atmospheric pressure.
2. Water cannot sustain an absolute pressure below its vapour pressure. Where a full-pipe solution would require one, the column separates at the point of lowest pressure; the pressure there equals the vapour pressure, the line downstream of that point runs partly full under vapour and its drop adds no suction.
3. Pump and valve states change only through the operations recorded in the operating log. A pump in AUTO-standby starts automatically only if PI-100 falls below 2.0 bar g, and only an operator stops a running pump.
